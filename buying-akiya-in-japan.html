Buying an Akiya in Japan: Checklist for Overseas Buyers

Exploring an affordable vacant house or second home in Tohoku? A low asking price is only the beginning. Compare properties across Aomori, Iwate, Miyagi, Akita, Yamagata and Fukushima with these practical questions in mind.

1. Confirm the property and ownership

  • Verify the seller's authority, title and any rights or restrictions affecting the land and building.
  • Check boundaries, access rights, zoning and permitted uses with qualified local professionals.
  • Ask whether the property is still available; publicly sourced listings may change.

2. Inspect before committing

  • Assess roof, structure, leaks, damp, pests, plumbing and electrical systems.
  • Get estimates for repairs and any necessary upgrades from appropriate contractors.
  • Check water, wastewater, heating and internet arrangements.

3. Understand the total cost

  • Allow for taxes, registration, professional fees, insurance and renovation.
  • Budget for utilities, periodic visits, repairs and ongoing property care.
  • Seek independent legal and tax advice, particularly for cross-border ownership and payments.

4. Plan for Tohoku's seasons

Ask about winter road access, snow clearance, frozen pipes and how the home will be checked while unoccupied. Conditions vary significantly by area.

5. Arrange local support

Overseas owners may benefit from on-site condition checks, photographic reporting and coordination with local tradespeople. NexT DooR shares public-source property information and local field-support options.

This guide is general information, not legal, tax or real-estate brokerage advice. Property availability, prices and rules require independent verification.
