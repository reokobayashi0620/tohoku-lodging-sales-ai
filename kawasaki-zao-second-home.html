MIYAGI · TOHOKU · JAPAN SECOND HOME

Why consider Kawasaki Town and the Zao area for a second home in Japan?

For overseas buyers drawn to the Japanese countryside, Kawasaki Town in Miyagi Prefecture offers an alternative to a crowded resort setting: mountains, hot-spring communities and proximity to the wider Sendai region.

What makes the area worth exploring?

Mountains and seasons

The Zao region is known for mountain scenery and seasonal outdoor activities. Conditions and access vary by exact address and season.

Hot-spring communities

Aone Onsen is within Kawasaki Town, while Togatta Onsen is in neighboring Zao Town. Both provide useful reference points when comparing locations.

Sendai region access

Kawasaki Town is connected to the broader Sendai region by road. Confirm actual driving routes, winter conditions and public transport for any property.

Second home or investment property?

A private retreat and an income-generating property require different checks. For a second home, prioritize year-round access, maintenance, heating and how frequently you will visit. For an investment, independently verify demand, running costs, permitted uses, applicable regulations and management arrangements. Rental income is not guaranteed.

Five checks before buying

  1. Building condition: inspect the roof, structure, water systems, insulation and signs of moisture.
  2. Winter access: confirm snow clearance, road ownership and parking.
  3. Land and rules: review title, boundaries, zoning, development restrictions and any private community covenants.
  4. Full costs: budget for inspections, repairs, taxes, utilities and ongoing upkeep—not just the asking price.
  5. Purchase support: consult appropriate licensed real-estate and legal professionals for transaction matters.

This is an area guide, not a claim that a specific property is available. Listings, prices, travel times and permitted uses must be verified individually.
