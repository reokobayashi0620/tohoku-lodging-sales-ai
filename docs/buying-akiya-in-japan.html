Buying an Akiya in Japan: Guide for Overseas Buyers

An akiya is a vacant or underused home in Japan. Public akiya-bank information can be a useful starting point, but availability, condition and transaction requirements should always be confirmed with the original source and appropriate licensed professionals.

How to start

  1. Choose your preferred area in Tohoku and set a realistic total budget.
  2. Review public-source property information and confirm that the property is still available.
  3. Arrange a local field check for the building, surroundings and visible repair needs.
  4. Discuss legal, contractual, registration, tax and transaction matters with the appropriate qualified professionals.
  5. Plan renovation, maintenance and local management before purchase where needed.

Why a local field check matters

Photos and public listings do not always show current condition, access, surroundings or visible deterioration. NexT DooR / officeMK can provide on-the-ground field support in Tohoku, including photo/video reporting and practical condition checks, and can coordinate purchase inquiries with appropriate partners.

Costs beyond the property price

Overseas buyers should plan for more than the advertised price. Depending on the property and transaction, professional fees, taxes, registration, repairs, utilities, insurance, maintenance and ongoing management may also apply. Confirm the actual costs for a specific property with qualified professionals before making a decision.
